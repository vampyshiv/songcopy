Get your set out of your library and onto your gear

You have a set list. Somewhere in a library of thousands of songs are the files for it. dubplate finds them all at once, tells you which ones you don't have, and copies the rest where you need them: your DJ software's folder, or a USB stick for the club's decks.

What dubplate does

  1. You give it a listA spreadsheet, a Spotify playlist export, a text file, or rows pasted from Excel or Google Sheets.
  2. It finds every song in your music libraryIt reads your library's file and folder names, and it knows a Radio Edit isn't the Extended Mix you asked for.
  3. You check what it foundEach song gets a colour: Found Best guess Other version only Not found. You can listen to any of them first.
  4. It copies them where you wantTo a folder (for Traktor, Serato, rekordbox, VirtualDJ…), to a plain USB stick for the decks, or to a rekordbox USB with BPM, key and waveforms.

It only ever copies. Your library is never changed: nothing is moved, deleted or overwritten. Load the same list again next week and only the songs you've added since are copied.

dubplate is in alphaIt works, but it's still being tested. Always try a stick or a folder on your own gear before you rely on it at a gig.

What you need

  • A Windows or Linux computer. dubplate is one small program with nothing else to install.
  • Your music library on that computer, on an external drive, or on a home server.
  • A web browser. dubplate opens its own page in it; nothing goes to the internet except the update check.
