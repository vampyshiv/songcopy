Get started

Your first set, step by step

This walks through one whole set: from a list of songs to files ready to play. If you haven't installed dubplate yet, do that first.

Before you startHave dubplate installed and its page open in your browser, and have your set list ready. Any of these works: a spreadsheet, a Spotify playlist export, a text file with one Artist - Title per line. Not sure? See Song lists.

1. Give it your list

The page has three numbered steps. In step 1, Your song list:

  • Drop your file onto the box, or click the box to choose it.
  • Or click …or paste a list and paste rows straight from Excel or Google Sheets, or type lines like Daft Punk - One More Time. Then click Find these songs.

Underneath is If a song exists as both FLAC and MP3, use. If your library has some songs in both formats, this picks which one is copied. FLAC is the best quality; MP3 is smaller.

2. Check what it found

In a moment every song on your list appears with a colour:

ColourWhat it meansIs it copied?
FoundThe exact song.Yes
Best guessA close match, such as a typo or a different spelling. Listen to check it.Yes. Untick it to leave it out.
Other version onlyYou asked for one version (say the Extended Mix) but only have another (the radio edit, a remix).No, unless you tick it.
Not foundIt isn't in your library.No
  • Press ▶ next to any song to hear it.
  • Own more than one version? The menu under the song lists them; pick one to use it instead.
  • Click a coloured count at the top (like 3 best guess) to see only those songs.
  • Red songs show Did you mean suggestions, and Search the library myself… lets you look for one by hand.

Missing songs? Don't worry about them yet: copy what you have now, and see Missing songs afterwards.

3. Choose where they go

Step 3, Where should they go?, has three choices:

  • 📁 Copy to a folder for DJ software on a laptop. More about this
  • 💾 Make a USB for the decks for a simple stick for CDJs/XDJs. More about this
  • 🎛 Rekordbox USB for a stick with BPM, key and waveforms on the decks. More about this

Pick one, then choose the place: type it in, or click Browse… and pick a folder (or your USB stick). dubplate remembers it for next time.

4. Copy

Click the big button: Copy 24 songs (or Make USB with 24 songs, or Make the rekordbox playlist with 24 songs). A progress bar shows each song as it goes. When it's done, a green box says what was copied, and the copied songs disappear from the list.

Next time is quicker. dubplate remembers what it copied to each place. Get the missing songs, load the same list again, and only those new songs are offered. Click ✓ already copied to see the rest.

Every option explains itself

Each option on the page has a small ? next to it. Hover over it (or tap it on a phone) for a short explanation. How dubplate works at the top of the page is a one-screen summary.
