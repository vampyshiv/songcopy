When songs are missing

Some songs on your list aren't in your library, or only in a different version. Here's how to check, how to keep a shopping list, and how to fetch them through Soulseek if you use it.

First, check it really is missing

  • Not found songs show Did you mean suggestions. If one is right, click it and the song turns green.
  • Search the library myself… under a song opens a search box: type a few words of the artist or title.
  • Added the song to your library just now? Click Rescan library at the top of the page.
  • Other version only means you own a different version (say the radio edit instead of the extended mix). Tick it to copy what you have, or pick another version from its menu.

Keep a shopping list

Click ⬇ Download the missing songs as a CSV. You get a spreadsheet of every missing and other-version song, with the closest thing your library has next to each. Take it to your record store, Bandcamp or Beatport.

When you've got the songs into your library, load the same list again. Everything you already copied is hidden, so only the new songs are copied.

Fetch them through Soulseek

slskd is a Soulseek client that runs as a web app. If you use it, dubplate can search Soulseek for your missing songs and queue the files you approve. Download only music you have the right to.

Connect slskd once

  1. Open ⚙ Settings in dubplateAt the top of the page. Find Soulseek (slskd).
  2. Enter slskd's addressThe address you open slskd with in your browser, for example http://localhost:5030.
  3. Make an API keyClick Make a key for me. dubplate shows a few lines with your new key. Click Copy these lines.
  4. Add the lines to slskdIn slskd's web page, go to System → Options → Edit (or edit its slskd.yml file) and paste them. If there's already an api_keys: line, add only the dubplate: part under it. Save, and restart slskd if it asks. 
    web:
  authentication:
    api_keys:
      dubplate:
        key: your-new-key
        role: readwrite
    The key needs role: readwrite: a read-only key can't search or download.
  5. Click Test connectionIt shows slskd's version and whether it's logged in to Soulseek.
  6. Add slskd's download folder to your libraryUnder Also look in, so downloaded songs are found. dubplate offers it with one click when it can see that folder. If slskd runs in Docker, add the same folder with + Add a folder… instead.
  7. Click Save settings

Find and download

  1. Click 🔎 Find on SoulseekAbove the list (for all missing songs) or under one song.
  2. Check the picksFor each song dubplate shows its best find, ticked if it's an exact match. It uses the same rules as for your library, so it won't pick a radio edit when you asked for the extended mix. It prefers lossless files, then MP3 320, skips anything under 192 kbps, and prefers people who can send it now. Other results are in the menu under the pick.
  3. Click ⬇ Download from SoulseekNothing downloads until you do.
  4. Wait for the songs to turn greenProgress shows under each song ("waiting in queue", "downloading 45%"). When downloads finish, dubplate reads your library again and the songs are ready to copy.

slskd does the downloading, with its own settings and limits. Your API key stays on your computer and isn't shown again after you save it.
