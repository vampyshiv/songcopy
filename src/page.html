Pick the USB stick (or a folder on it) above. Songs go into one folder, numbered in your list's order, and anything an older deck (XDJ-1000, CDJ-900/2000) can't play is converted; tags and artwork are kept. On the deck, browse the stick with its folder view. Use a stick formatted as FAT32, the one format every Pioneer deck reads.
